Keep the story of your library.

A personal record of the games you care about, with room for the ones you will return to.

01 / ORGANIZE

Find your next game.

Search titles and platforms. See what is planned, in progress, paused, finished or dropped.

02 / REMEMBER

Record your experience.

Keep personal ratings, playtime, completion dates, platinum goals and a few notes for later.

03 / KEEP

Your library stays local.

Your records live in SQLite on your computer. The core app works offline and needs no account.

A SMALL APP, BUILT IN THE OPEN

Useful now.
Room to grow.

Game Tracker is in early development. The first working slice focuses on a persistent personal library.

  • Available in source Library, ratings, time and progress
  • Next Backup, spreadsheet import and sagas
  • Planned Cover lookup and achievement integrations

Designed to stay lightweight. Memory and CPU budgets are tracked as the app grows; full workload benchmarks are still pending.
