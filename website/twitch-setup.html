  1. 01

    TWITCH DEVELOPER CONSOLE

    Register your application.

    Sign in to the Twitch developer console ↗. Verify your account email and enable two-factor authentication, then open Applications and choose Register Your Application.

    Portuguese labels: Aplicativos → Registrar seu aplicativo.

    You are creating an application, not an extension. Choose a unique name, such as Game Tracker IGDB — your username.

    Name
    A unique name for your own application
    OAuth Redirect URL
    http://localhost — then click Add
    Category
    Select the integration category that fits your use

    IGDB does not use the redirect URL for this connection; localhost fills the registration requirement. Follow Twitch's account-verification prompts yourself.

  2. 02

    THE IMPORTANT CHOICE

    Choose Confidential.

    Set Client Type to Confidential before creating the application. IGDB requires application credentials, including a Client Secret.

    ●ConfidentialGenerates a Client Secret for this IGDB connection.
    ○PublicDoes not provide the secret this setup needs.

    Portuguese labels: Tipo de cliente → Confidencial.

    If you already created a Public application and Twitch locks its type, register a new Confidential application with a different unique name.

  3. 03

    APPLICATION DETAILS

    Get the Client ID and secret.

    Return to Applications, find your application and select Manage. Copy its Client ID, then select New Secret to generate a Client Secret.

    Portuguese labels: Gerenciar → Novo segredo.

    Keep the secret private. Paste it only into the desktop app. Generating another secret invalidates the previous one, so update your connection if you replace it.

    No secret button? Check that the application is Confidential, not Public.

  4. 04

    BACK IN GAME TRACKER

    Connect from Settings.

    Open the gear at the bottom of the desktop app's sidebar. Go to Settings → Integrations → Twitch / IGDB, enter your Client ID and Client Secret, then press Connect to IGDB.

    Settings→Integrations→Twitch / IGDB

    A successful connection shows Connected to IGDB for this session. The password field clears when submitted. The app keeps its access token in memory; it does not save your Client Secret to the library.

    Closing the app disconnects the session. You can also choose Disconnect IGDB. Reconnect after restarting or when the session expires. Manual game tracking continues to work offline.

  5. 05

    PICK THE RIGHT EDITION

    Find a game and save its cover.

    Open a game's edit popup by clicking its name. Expand Find a game & cover on IGDB, enter a title and press Search IGDB. Compare the title, release year and platforms before choosing an edition.

    Choose the result and press Save game. The cover is cached on your computer for offline viewing. Your personal status, notes, platform and rating stay unchanged. Existing titles are preserved; new games can use the catalog title.

    Community scores are labeled IGDB on a 100-point scale. Your own ratings stay on the 1–5 scale. The integration does not fetch written reviews or automatically match every imported game.

WHEN SOMETHING DOESN'T MATCH

A few common fixes.

I cannot find New Secret.

Check the Client Type. If it is Public and cannot be changed, register a new Confidential application. Manage the new application and generate its secret there.

The app cannot connect.

Check that the Client ID and secret belong to the same Twitch application. Use the newest secret if you regenerated it. Check your internet connection, then try again from Settings. The error should not be shared together with credentials or screenshots of the secret.

I connected, but the library still has placeholders.

Connect first, then search and select an edition in each game's edit popup and save it. A connection alone does not pick editions or download covers for your whole library.

The connection disappeared after restarting.

That is expected in this version. Credentials are session-only. Reconnect in Settings; already cached covers and saved game records remain on your computer.

Where do I enter a Steam key?

Steam synchronization is still planned. Its Settings card is marked Coming soon and does not collect keys in this version. Twitch/IGDB supplies the current game and cover lookup.
